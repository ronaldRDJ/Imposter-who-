How to Play

  1. Gather 3 or more friends around one mobile phone.
  2. Type everyone's name into the list.
  3. Pass the phone around. Everyone secretly looks at their card.
  4. Innocents see the real Secret Word. The Imposter sees an alert and a strategic 1-word fallback hint.
  5. Take turns going around in a circle saying exactly one word related to your card.
  6. Discuss, find out who gave a suspicious clue, and vote them off!
